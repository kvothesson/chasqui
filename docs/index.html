Real-time voice translation

Speak your language.
Hear theirs in yours.

A small window on your desktop that translates speech as it happens: your calls in any app, both ways, or any video playing on your PC. Open source. Your own OpenAI key. You pay by the minute, nothing else.

$ pip install chasqui-translate

then chasqui ui and press START

Three ways to use it

media

their video → your ears

YouTube, a stream, a lecture, anything your PC plays. You hear it in your language while it plays.

$0.034/min · about $2 an hour

headset

you ⇄ them, any app

You speak your language and they hear theirs. They answer and you hear yours in your earphones. Zero config: it swaps your Windows default mic while it runs and gives it back on stop. Meet, Zoom, Teams, Discord.

$0.068/min · two directions

meet

you ⇄ them, through a virtual mic

The same two-way call, wired by hand: you pick the virtual cable as the mic inside Meet or Zoom, and your Windows defaults stay untouched.

$0.068/min · two directions

What it costs

$0.034 / minute

chasqui uses OpenAI's gpt-realtime-translate, which is billed by the minute of audio, not by tokens. No subscription, no account with us: the bill comes from your own OpenAI key, and the window shows a running total while you talk.

A two-way call runs two streams at once, so it costs double. OpenAI also prices live transcription ($0.017/min) separately, and it is not settled whether that stacks on top; chasqui's meter counts it, so the figure on screen is the high end. Prices as of July 2026.

What you need

  • Python 3.11 or newer
  • An OpenAI API key with access to gpt-realtime-translate
  • Windows, for the audio modes
  • VB-Audio Cable (free) for the audio modes, plus Stereo Mix (or Hi-Fi Cable) for two-way calls
  • 12 languages: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin, Arabic, Russian, Hindi
  • Linux and macOS: text mode only, for now
